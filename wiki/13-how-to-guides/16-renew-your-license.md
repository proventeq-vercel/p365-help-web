# How to Renew Your Proventeq365 License

Your license controls how long Proventeq365 can be used and which features are enabled. To renew it, open **Systems** → **License**, enter the new license key you received from Proventeq, and click **Upload License**.

## Before you begin

- Get the new license key from your Proventeq contact. The key is in GUID format, for example `xxxxxxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxxxxxxxxx`.

## Steps

1. Open **Systems** → **License**.
2. Under **License Overview**, check the current **License Status**, the **Valid From** and **Valid To** dates and the **Enabled Features**.
3. Under **Upload a New License**, enter the new license key.
4. Click **Upload License**. Proventeq365 validates the key and applies the new license.

## Result

The **License Overview** shows the new validity dates and enabled features. The menu options available in Proventeq365 depend on the modules your license enables.
