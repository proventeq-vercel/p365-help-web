# How to Choose Which Notifications You Get by Email

Proventeq365 can tell you about each event in the app, by email, or both. You choose this per event on the **Preferences** tab of the Notifications screen. Your choices apply only to you.

## Before you begin

- Any signed-in user can change their own notification preferences. No particular role or license feature is needed.
- Email is offered only for failures, approvals and changes to your workspace access. Progress and success events are in-app only.
- Emails are sent only when your administrator has set up a notification mailbox for Proventeq365. See [Notification Mailbox](../12-deployment-prerequisites/07-run-post-deployment-script.md#notification-mailbox-optional).

## Steps

1. Click the **bell** icon next to your name at the top of the screen.
2. Open the **Preferences** tab.
3. Find the event under its **Notification Group**, for example **Data refresh**, **Policies** or **Provisioning**.
4. Tick **Email** for each event you want by email, and clear it for the ones you don't.
5. Tick or clear **Push notifications** to choose whether the event appears in the app and your inbox.
6. To change a whole group at once, use the checkbox on the group's row.

## Result

From now on, each event reaches you the way you chose. Events whose **Email** box is unavailable are never sent by email. If you haven't changed a preference, you receive the event both in the app and, where offered, by email.
